# VOX Demo Video Script (2:30 – 2:50)

### 0:00 – 0:20 | Introduction & Problem
- **Visual**: Show a dense, technical research article in Chrome with small text.
- **Narrator**: "We consume massive amounts of information through long articles, tutorials, and documentation every day. Reading everything takes time, and current AI chat tools force us to constantly read text and switch tabs. Meet VOX: Your Voice Companion for the Internet. The internet, hands-free."

### 0:20 – 0:45 | Launching VOX in Chrome Side Panel
- **Visual**: Click the VOX extension icon. The polished VOX side panel smoothly appears on the right side of the screen without occluding the article.
- **Narrator**: "VOX lives right alongside your browsing session in the Chrome Side Panel. Notice the capability strip at the top: it checks your AI inference readiness, microphone status, speech synthesis, and network status in real time."

### 0:45 – 1:20 | "Teach Me This Page" & Spoken AI Output
- **Visual**: Click the prominent "Teach me this page" button. Show the waveform animating. The response appears with a "Grounded in: [Page Title]" tag, and VOX begins speaking the overview aloud.
- **Narrator**: "With one click or voice command, VOX extracts the main readable text from the active page, strips away menus and ads, and constructs an audio-friendly breakdown. Listen to how naturally it explains the core thesis."
- **Action**: Click "Mute Audio" to pause speech, then click the replay button.

### 1:20 – 1:50 | Voice Push-to-Talk & Conversational Follow-Up
- **Visual**: Click the "Voice" mic button. Speak: "Can you explain the main analogy in simpler terms?" The interim transcript appears live in the text box. The user taps Send.
- **Narrator**: "VOX supports natural push-to-talk speech input. You can preview and edit your transcript before sending. VOX maintains conversation context across turns, answering follow-up questions while keeping external page text strictly separated as reference data."

### 1:50 – 2:15 | Interactive Spoken Quiz Mode
- **Visual**: Click the "Quiz me" chip. VOX generates a targeted question grounded in the article and reads it aloud. The user speaks their answer. VOX evaluates it and provides spoken feedback.
- **Narrator**: "VOX doesn't just summarize—it tests your comprehension. In Quiz mode, it asks one question at a time, listens to your answer, and gives immediate, grounded feedback."

### 2:15 – 2:35 | Truthful Hybrid Inference Transparency
- **Visual**: Zoom in on the inference badge on the AI response: "On-Device (Nano)" or "Cloud Fallback". Click the "?" info button to show the latency and reason. Open the Settings modal and show the Hybrid AI inference preference options.
- **Narrator**: "Under the hood, VOX is powered by Firebase AI Logic. It uses hybrid inference: if Chrome has Gemini Nano downloaded locally, inference runs completely on-device for zero latency and private processing. When local resources are unavailable, it seamlessly falls back to cloud Gemini. VOX never fakes its status—the badge reflects the actual SDK response metadata."

### 2:35 – 2:50 | Closing & Value Proposition
- **Visual**: Return to side-by-side view of article and VOX.
- **Narrator**: "Voice-first, page-grounded, and hybrid AI native. That is VOX—learning from the internet, completely hands-free. Built for the Google Firebase #BuildHybridAI hackathon."
